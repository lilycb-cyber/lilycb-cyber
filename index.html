Our Story


The UNC Gardening Club was founded in 2010 by a group of passionate students who wanted to create a community for gardening enthusiasts on campus. Since then, we have grown into a vibrant club that welcomes students from all backgrounds and skill levels. Our members share a love for plants, sustainability, and the joy of gardening.

Our Goals


Our goal is to promote gardening and sustainability on campus, while also providing a fun and educational experience for our members. We welcome anyone who is passionate about gardening and environmental awareness.

What We Do


We organize regular gardening workshops, plant swaps, and community service projects. Our members also have the opportunity to participate in campus garden maintenance and beautification projects. We believe that gardening is not only a rewarding hobby, but also a way to connect with nature and promote a sustainable future.
